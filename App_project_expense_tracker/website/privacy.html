Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Expense Tracker app ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

The app collects only the information you provide:

  • Account information — your email address and authentication details when you create an account.
  • Financial data — the transactions, budgets, savings goals, debts, planned payments, and categories you record in the app.

2. How your data is stored

Your financial data is stored in a private, password-protected cloud database. It is accessible only to you when signed in to your verified account. Data is also cached locally on your device so the app works offline.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate your account and keep your data secure.
  • To sync your data across your devices.
  • To provide the app's features (tracking, budgets, reports, and exports).
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising or profiling.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections and restricted access, to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your rights

You can export your data at any time from the app (CSV or PDF), edit or delete your records, and contact us to request account deletion. You may also stop using the app and delete it from your device at any time.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@example.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
